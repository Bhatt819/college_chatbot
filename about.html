Our Story

Established in 1993 as Graphic Era Institute, Graphic Era Hill University has evolved into one of India's premier educational institutions. What began as a small computer training center has now transformed into a full-fledged university, recognized for its academic excellence and research contributions.

From humble beginnings to a NAAC 'A' Grade accredited university, our journey reflects our commitment to providing quality education and creating leaders of tomorrow.

Located in the picturesque city of Dehradun, nestled in the foothills of the Himalayas, our campus provides an ideal environment for learning and innovation. With state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and a focus on holistic development, we prepare students to excel in a rapidly changing global landscape.

Today, GEHU stands tall as a symbol of educational excellence, with over 25,000 alumni making significant contributions across the globe in various fields including technology, business, research, and social development.
